### Audience
Product managers (why/what), Product owner (how/when), Team (Dev), Stakeholders, inter alia.

## High-level requirements

* The new Integrated Systems must be capable of modernizing the current isolated systems due silo software, with an integrated systems solution (Integrated Systems) that consists a comprehensive management platform and an investment platform.
* The integrated platform must be Multi-tenancy architecture to support Multi-tenants.
* And must be able to evolve in accordance with the evolving needs of the business.

## Systems Log-in & Sign-up

* Management: Login (without sign up).
* Activities: Login (without sign up).
* Investment: Login with sign up.

## Roles

* Management platform roles: Collaborators, Clients, Managers.
* Investment platform role: Partners

## High-level user stories

* Collaborator can view the list of activities planned for the day.
* Collaborator can mark activities as completed once they have finished them.
* Collaborator see earned previous month and accumulated in current month.

* Clients can view the payment history

* Managers can manage clients.
* Managers can add and manage payments made by clients
* Managers can manage Guarantees.
* Managers can see the Balance sheet history.
