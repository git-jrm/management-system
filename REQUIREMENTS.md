# Requirements management system

## Audience

Product managers (what/why), Product owner (when/how), Team (what/how), Stakeholders (why/when), inter alia.

## High-level requirements

New Management System must be capable of:

* Modernizing the platform by replacing the current isolated system due to siloed software.
* Must be Multi-tenancy architecture to support Multi-customer.
* And must be able to evolve in accordance with modules architecture aproach.
* Roles: Clients rol and Manager rol.

## Acceptance criteria

### Managers stories

* Managers can manage clients.
* Managers can add and manage payments made by clients
* Managers can manage Guarantees.
* Managers can see the Balance sheet history.

### customer stories

* Clients can view the payment history
